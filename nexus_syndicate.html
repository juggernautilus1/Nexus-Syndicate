Whenever the combined Spanning score passes a multiple of 10, the acting player takes 3 pyramids (recruitment pool first, then the popularity pools) and places them on any sectors. Passing two multiples at once gives 6.
Once per game, at the start or end of your turn, you may move any one influence marker to another sector that already has a marker. This needs one pyramid of each colour in your popularity pool, which go to the recruitment pool.
